# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want everyone to be able to take part in this project without harassment,
whatever their age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We act and interact in ways that keep this a welcoming, open and healthy
community.

## Our standards

Behavior that helps the community:

- Being kind and patient, especially with people new to MCP, OAuth or open source.
- Respecting different viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving feedback that is specific and useful, and taking it gracefully.
- Owning mistakes, apologizing, and learning from them.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and the people who use it.

Behavior that is not acceptable:

- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing someone else's private information, such as an address or email, without permission.
- Anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces: issues, pull requests,
comments, discussions and any other channel run by the project. It also applies
when someone is representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

The maintainer is responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and
may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions
that do not follow this Code of Conduct. They will explain the reason when they
do.

### Reporting

To report a problem, use GitHub's private reporting: open the repository's
**Security** tab, choose **Report a vulnerability**, and say in the first line
that it is a conduct report, not a security issue. This reaches the maintainer
and keeps the report private. You can also contact the maintainer
([@amir1824](https://github.com/amir1824)) through the options on their GitHub
profile.

All reports are reviewed promptly and in good faith. The maintainer will respect
the privacy and safety of the person who reported.

### Consequences

The maintainer will follow these steps, in order of severity:

1. **Correction.** A private, written note that explains what was inappropriate
   and why. A public apology may be asked for.
2. **Warning.** A warning that further incidents will have consequences,
   including a pause on interaction with the people involved.
3. **Temporary ban.** A time-limited ban from all project spaces.
4. **Permanent ban.** A permanent ban for a pattern of unacceptable behavior or
   for harassment.
